Legal & Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

AI YOUTUBER is currently a prototype. Features may be incomplete, and generated content may be inaccurate. Please review all content before using or publishing it.

What the prototype does

AI YOUTUBER is intended to help users plan and create YouTube content. The current prototype includes account access and a chat interface. Some displayed features are not yet connected or available.

Information and account data

The prototype uses Supabase for account authentication. If you create an account, your email address and authentication data are processed by Supabase to provide that service. Do not enter passwords, private information, or confidential material into the chat.

AI-generated content

AI-generated ideas, text, audio, or other content may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable. You are responsible for checking content, permissions, and accuracy before publishing.

YouTube and third-party services

YouTube features and integrations are not currently connected in this prototype. If they are added, their use will be subject to applicable third-party terms and policies.

Privacy and data controls

This prototype does not yet provide a complete in-app process for exporting or deleting all account data. Avoid using it for sensitive information. Before public launch, the service needs a reviewed privacy policy and a clear way to request data access or deletion.

Contact

For questions about this prototype, contact the project owner through the contact method provided when the service is publicly launched.
